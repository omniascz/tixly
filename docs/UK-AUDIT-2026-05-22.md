## 0. Executive summary

### Tržní landscape UK 2026

UK ticketing trh je v 2026 v zásadním regulačním přechodu:

- **#1 Ticketmaster UK** (Live Nation Entertainment) — odhad **~70 %** primárního trhu velkých arén/stadionů, dominantní position pod stálým antitrust + parliamentary scrutiny (CMA, House of Commons Library briefing 2026/01).
- **#2 See Tickets UK** — Eventim-owned od června 2024 ($323M deal s Vivendi), silný napříč theatre/comedy/festivals/sports.
- **AXS UK** (AEG) — vertikální moat top arén v UK: **The O2 (London), OVO Arena Wembley, OVO Hydro Glasgow, AO Arena Manchester, First Direct Arena Leeds, Utilita Arena Newcastle**. Mobile-only Mobile ID jako default entry.
- **DICE** (Fever od června 2025) — mobile-first indie/club/electronic scene, žádné PDF tickety, animated rotating QR, hard anti-touting filozofie.
- **Eventbrite** + **Skiddle** — self-service segment. Eventbrite global cross-vertikál, Skiddle UK clubbing/festival vertikála s vlastním rep-network sales channel.

**Klíčový regulatory shock:** **King's Speech 13. května 2026** ohlásil **draft Ticket Tout Ban Bill** + **Sporting Events Bill**. Resale **above face value bude ilegální**, **service fees na resale platformách budou capped**, CMA dostane **fines až 10 % global turnover** přes Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCCA). Existující Digital Economy Act 2017 už zakazuje bots a vyžaduje unique ticket numbers v listinz.

### Tři universal patterns napříč všemi 5 platformami

1. **Face-value resale s primary-DB link** = UK standard u všech promotér-respektujících platforem (Ticketmaster Fan-to-Fan, fanSALE.co.uk, AXS Official Resale, DICE Wait List). Skiddle resale neoficiální, Eventbrite formálně nemá. **Tixly nemá nic.**
2. **Mobile-first ticket delivery + rotating QR** — SafeTix (TM), AXS Mobile ID (59s rotation), DICE animated QR + 2h-pre-event activation = anti-screenshot anti-scalp default. Tixly má rotating QR/Wallet, ale není default pro top eventy.
3. **All-in pricing / fee absorption choice** — Eventim Light UK (8 % flat, promotér volí absorb/pass), Eventbrite UK (6.95 % + £0.59), DICE (~12-15 % buyer-paid all-in v ceně), Skiddle (10 % + £0.25 default). Ticketmaster naopak fees fragmented (service + processing + delivery). Tixly v3 má 5-8 % CZ/SK.

### Tři strategické mezery, které Tixly může využít

1. **CZ/SK lokalizace pro UK promotéry** — žádná UK platforma nepodporuje CS/SK. Tixly může nabídnout UK-based promoterům CEE expansion-kit (CS/SK frontend, CZK/EUR pricing, GoPay/ComGate native).
2. **Compliance-ready resale s face-value cap** — Tixly aktuálně nemá resale wůbec. **Naopak výhoda:** může implementovat čistě "UK Ticket Tout Ban Bill compliant" resale od začátku, bez legacy markup-resale baggage (které musí StubHub/Viagogo/secondary teď refactorovat). Eventim fanSALE.co.uk model je referenční (face value MAX, listing zdarma).
3. **Theatre subscription pro West End mid-tier** — ATG Plus, Delfont Mackintosh group bookings dominují, ale **closed-loop** (ne dostupné indie produkcím). Tixly se s "Event Bundle" patterny (Eventim Light style) může pozicovat na **regional theatres + touring productions** mimo Big 3 (ATG, Delfont, LW).

### Co Tixly už dělá lépe než většina UK konkurence

- **Multi-tenant promotér model** s branded storefronty `/p/[promoter]` (jen Eventim Inhouse + Eventbrite Pro mají paralelu)
- **CS/SK/EN full lokalizace** + CZK+EUR multi-currency
- **GoPay/ComGate provider abstrakce** (CEE-native)
- **DSA report queue + Notifications center + Status page incidents** (EU compliance, zero UK platform exposes public)
- **Refund flow uvolňuje seats** automaticky
- **Insurance Stornoschutz buyer UI** (jen Eventim AT, AXS UK přes 3rd-party Allianz nabízí; Tixly už implementoval per memory)
- **Print Marketing QR s attribution + cookie pickup** (Tixly v3 specific feature, no UK parity)
- **Self-service signup** (TM/AXS sales-led, See Tickets enterprise-led)

## 1. Ticketmaster UK (Live Nation Entertainment)

**URL:** [ticketmaster.co.uk](https://www.ticketmaster.co.uk/) | B2B: [business.ticketmaster.co.uk](https://business.ticketmaster.co.uk/) | Developer: [developer.ticketmaster.com](https://developer.ticketmaster.com/) | EN-GB + multi-domain | Multi-currency GBP/EUR/USD/CAD/AUD via global TM platform

### Tržní pozice

- **Market share:** ~70 %+ UK primary ticketing (Yale antitrust report, parliamentary briefing 2026/01)
- **Parent:** Live Nation Entertainment (merger 2010 s Live Nation Inc.)
- **B2B brand:** **Ticketmaster Business** + **TM1** (enterprise platform suite)
- **Scope:** Stadia, arenas, festivals, theatres (mid-large), sports (Premier League partial). Pod parliamentary scrutiny od Oasis 2024 dynamic pricing incident.

### Pricing / Fee model

**Buyer-facing fees (UK, ne transparent public schedule — vary by event):**
- **Service fee** (variable, % of face value, typicky £4-£20 per ticket)
- **Order Processing Fee** (flat per order, £2.75-£3.75 typically)
- **Delivery Fee** (mobile = £0, postal £2.50+)
- Pops and Hisses (UK consumer analysis 2025/03): aggregate fees mohou činit **až 75 %** of face value v extrémech.
- **Fan-to-Fan Resale** fee: ~15 % seller fee + 10-20 % buyer fee (face value exchange = 0 % fee)
- **Dynamic Pricing** ("Platinum tickets", "In-Demand tickets") — algorithmic, Oasis 2024 escalation paused but legally OK pod current law

**Promotér fee (B2B):** Sales-led, custom contracts, ne self-service. Industry odhad 3-7 % commission + venue exclusivity rights, ale **netransparentní**.

### Buyer features

- **Search & discovery:** [ticketmaster.co.uk](https://www.ticketmaster.co.uk/) homepage, native app iOS+Android, push notifs pre-sale alerts
- **Checkout:** Karty (Visa/MC/Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, **Klarna BNPL** (Pay in 3 / Pay in 30), wallet credit
- **SafeTix encrypted mobile ticket** — dynamicky obnovující se barcode + live animation, screen recording/screenshot blocked, identity-linked
- **Apple Wallet + Google Wallet** integration native
- **Verified Fan** queue system pro top eventy (anti-bot waitlist + lottery)
- **Refund policy:** 14-day cooling-off NEPLATÍ pro tickety (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 §28 výjimka). Event cancellation = guaranteed refund (face value + fees) automaticky na original payment method. Postponement = ticket valid, buyer-initiated change = ne (kromě výjimečných promoter policies)

### Promotér features (TM1 suite)

- **TM1 Events** — event creation, custom floor edits, VIP management, focal point selling order, drag&drop seating editor s presety
- **TM1 Sales** — real-time inventory, dynamic pricing, hold management
- **TM1 Entry** — venue entry control real-time, VIP routing, all-points-ingress monitoring
- **TM1 Reports** — analytics + insights
- **TM1 Marketing** — audience engagement, presale orchestration, Verified Fan codes
- **Host** — legacy venue platform (mainframe-era, still active for large venues)

### Anti-scalp / Resale

- **SafeTix** = patent-pending rotating barcode (Ticketmaster + AEG cross-license s AXS Mobile ID architecture similar)
- **Fan-to-Fan Resale** = official capped resale na **same primary platform**, face value + fees max (může jít i nad pokud event-level pravidlo)
- Live animation + dynamic refresh = screenshot ≠ valid ticket
- **Capped resale exchange** (House of Commons Library briefing 2026/01 přímo citováno jako reference model)

### API ecosystem

- **developer.ticketmaster.com** = Partner API (gated, OAuth), **Discovery API** (public, search + listings), **Commerce API** (partner), **Presence API** (scanner SDK), **SafeTix API** (partner integration)
- Webhooks: gated, sales-led
- iFrame embed: existuje (event widget), gated B2B
- White-label: TM1 Inhouse pro nejvyšší tier (Wembley Stadium custom integration)

### Festivals & special

- **British Summer Time Hyde Park, All Points East, Reading & Leeds** = primary TM eventy
- Multi-day support, accessibility companion tickets (UK 2026 update — new scheme)
- Camping/glamping = add-on tickets, ne nativní inventory

### Compliance

- UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018
- Consumer Rights Act 2015 (resale disclosure: seat number, location, original face value, restrictions) — TM compliant
- Digital Economy Act 2017 anti-bot — TM má Verified Fan jako mitigation
- **DMCCA 2024 + Ticket Tout Ban Bill 2026** (draft) = chystaná legislativa, TM resale model už blízko compliance (face value cap selektivně, ne universal — bude muset adjust)

### Mezera pro Tixly

Tixly je v jiné lize (TM = enterprise + global). Reálná opportunity je **regional/mid-market UK promotéři** (kteří TM nepřijal nebo přerostli Eventbrite). Tixly self-service signup + modern UX + transparent pricing = mezera vs. TM1 (sales-led, gated, opacní fees).

## 2. See Tickets UK (Eventim Group od 2024)

## 4. DICE (Fever-owned od 06/2025)

**URL:** [dice.fm](https://dice.fm/) | B2B: [mio.dice.fm](https://mio.dice.fm/) | Developer: [partners-endpoint.dice.fm](https://partners-endpoint.dice.fm/graphql/docs/) | UK/EU/US, ne CEE | Mobile-first

- **Akvizice:** Fever akvíruje DICE 5. června 2025, $100M raise paralelně. DICE zůstává standalone brand.

**Buyer side (all-in pricing):**
- **~10-15 % booking fee** included in displayed price (no checkout surprise)

### Anti-scalp / Resale

- **Friend Transfer** (per-event toggle) — recipient musí mít DICE account, sender ztrácí všechna práva, **face value max** pravidlo enforced (sender bears payment offline, ne přes platformu)

### Festivals & special

- Multiple festivals UK (Outernet, Wide Awake, Field Day historically)
- Multi-day support
- Group features (Groups = friend-coordination + alerts)
- **Contact sync FOMO** + "Interested in" signal

### Compliance

- **UK Standard Ticket Terms** ([support.dice.fm/article/286-uk-standard-ticket-terms](https://support.dice.fm/article/286-standard-ticket-terms))

- DMCCA 2024 + Ticket Tout Ban Bill 2026 = DICE už **fully compliant** (face value cap je default, ne opt-in)

### Co DICE nemá vs. Tixly target

- Reserved seating maps (GA focus)
- Printed PDF tickets (filozoficky)
- Multi-currency CZ/SK locale
- Subscription / season tickets / theatre Abo
- Promotér white-label / branded UX
- GoPay / ComGate
- Full POS s hotovostí
- Public B2B pricing transparency

### Mezera pro Tixly

DICE je **closed ecosystem** = ne pro klassik divadlo/opera/sport (kde seating + transfer + PDF backup matter). Tixly **CZ/SK seated venues + theatre Abonnement target** je orthogonal target.

## 9. Strategický verdict — má smysl Tixly UK expansion?

### Pro

1. **First-mover gap pro CEE-native + EU-resident promotéry** v UK — žádná UK platforma nemá CS/SK + EUR/CZK
2. **DMCCA 2024 + Tout Ban Bill 2026** = regulatory reset → resale features Tixly buduje od nuly s clean compliance
3. **Eventbrite price competition viable** — Tixly 5-8 % vs. Eventbrite 6.95 % + £0.59 (% advantage at low ticket prices)
4. **Skiddle nemá native buyer app** + Eventbrite nemá resale → diferenciace prostor
5. **Brexit nezáleží pro digital services** (no goods customs) — Tixly self-hosted Vercel + Supabase UK region možný
6. **Mid-tier theatres + touring productions** mimo ATG / Delfont / LW = underserved (West End Big 3 closed; Eventim Inhouse drahý)

### Proti

1. **TM ~70 % market dominance** s SafeTix + Verified Fan + Live Nation venue exclusivity. Mid+ promotéři lock-in
2. **AXS exclusive AEG venues** = O2, OVO Hydro, OVO Arena Wembley, AO Arena, First Direct Arena, Utilita Arena Newcastle — top arena tier closed
3. **See Tickets / Eventim Light 8 %** je cenově comparable a brand má 25 let UK trust legacy
4. **DICE mobile-first filozofie + brand cult** = UK indie/club scene velmi loajální
5. **Skiddle rep network** = unique UK distribution channel který Tixly nemá
6. **Klarna / Apple Pay / Google Pay** must-haves — Tixly potřebuje payment provider expansion mimo GoPay/ComGate
7. **Theatre subscription = closed loop** (ATG / Delfont = own + LW Theatres)
8. **GBP payments + Stripe UK** = další provider integration
9. **VAT registration UK** + UK entity (post-Brexit no EU-wide VAT MOSS pro non-digital services)

### Verdict

**Tixly by mělo nejdřív dominate CZ + SK (kde je first-mover advantage confirmed)** a UK expansion zvážit jako **2027/Q1 milestone** za těchto podmínek:

1. **P0-1 resale + P0-2 friend transfer hotové** (Tout Ban Bill compliance from day one)
2. **P1-1 Klarna BNPL** integrované (UK buyer expectation)
3. **P1-2 native mobile app** (UK competitive parity)
4. **P1-3 Event Bundle / Theatre subscription** (regional theatres value prop)
5. **UK entity + VAT registration + GDPR DPO** setup
6. **Pilot s 1-2 regional UK promotery** (Curve Leicester, Brighton Dome, Komedia, mid-tier touring productions) before scale

**Brexit consideration:** Není legal blocker pro digital ticketing services. Data residency lze řešit Vercel UK region + Supabase eu-west-2. UK GDPR ≈ EU GDPR (parity preserved 2026). VAT 20 % standard = jednodušší než EU VAT MOSS.

**Reverse opportunity (UK promotéři → CZ/SK trh):** UK touring productions (West End musicals na turné, Edinburgh Fringe → Prague Fringe parallel) potřebují CZ/SK + GoPay ticketing. **Tato vrstva je realistická Q4 2026** bez nutnosti plné UK expansion.
