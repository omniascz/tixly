## 3. Feature conflict / overlap detection

### Subscription tabs: 10
- `subscriptions.packages` (Festabo+Wahl)
- `subscriptions.holders` + `package_events` + `redemptions` + `consumptions`
- `subscriptions.plans` + `subscriptions.subscriptions` (legacy SaaS-style — souběžně s packages model, ne konflikt protože package_id ≠ plan_id)
- Plus: `b2b.webhook_subscriptions`, `notifications.push_subscriptions`, `tickets.wallet_push_subscriptions`, `discovery_feed.feed_subscriptions` — všechno jiné scope, žádný overlap

### Voucher tabs: 2
- `marketing.gift_vouchers` (admin gen + buyer self-purchase) + `gift_voucher_redemptions`

### Pass tabs: 19
- Wide spread — `tickets.access_passes`, `marketing.flex_pass_settings`, `catalog.cross_pass_bundles`, atd.

### RSVP/Interest: 1
- `marketing.event_rsvps` (going/interested/declined + is_public) — single source

### Order schemas: 20
- `orders.orders` central, addons, invoices, refunds, transit_passes — clean separation

**Závěr**: Žádné kritické overlapy. Subscription `plans` (recurring SaaS) vs `packages` (theatre Festabo) jsou jiné business modely a sdílejí jen schema namespace.
